# 🛒 Zepto Sales & Customer Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project demonstrates the development of an interactive **Sales & Customer Analytics Dashboard** using **Microsoft Excel**.

The dashboard transforms raw transaction data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, modeling, KPI tracking, and interactive visualizations.

# Business Insights

### Monthly Revenue Trend
- Stable revenue across the reporting period
- Seasonal fluctuations identified
- Revenue peaks indicate successful promotional periods

### Product Category Analysis
- Baby Care generated the highest revenue
- Grocery & Staples performed consistently
- Beverage category has growth potential

### Payment Analysis
- UPI is the preferred payment method
- Digital payments dominate customer transactions

### City Analysis
- Delhi is the highest revenue contributor
- Metro cities drive business growth

### Product Analysis
- Top-selling products contribute a significant portion of total revenue
- Inventory optimization is essential

# Business Recommendations

- Increase inventory for high-performing categories
- Promote digital payment methods
- Improve delivery performance
- Expand operations in top-performing cities
- Introduce loyalty programs
